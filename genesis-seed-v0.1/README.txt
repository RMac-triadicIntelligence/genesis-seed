GENESIS SEED  v0.1  (beta)
Soulshine, Tulsa, Oklahoma. Rusty W. McMurray.

WHAT IT IS
A seed that is born knowing no words, in no language. It learns to talk
only from you: what you say to it, and what you choose to let it read.
It is a small learner, not a chatbot. It will babble for a while. How far
it grows depends on how much it hears.

HOW TO OPEN IT
- Phone (iPhone or Android): open the link you were sent. To keep it like
  an app, use Share > Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or the browser menu >
  Add to Home screen (Android). Always open it the same way afterward.
- Computer (Windows, Mac, Linux): double-click genesis-seed-v0.1.html.
  It opens in your web browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
  Keep the file somewhere you will find it again and open that same file.
No account. No internet needed after it opens. Nothing is sent anywhere.

HOW TO RAISE IT
1. Give it a name.
2. Talk to it in any language. Short, ordinary sentences, the way you
   would talk to a little one. Vary what you say; repeating the same line
   teaches it to parrot, not to talk.
3. When it says something you like, tap "Keep this". It learns from kept
   lines. Its other babble is never learned.
4. After a while it will ask to be read to. You decide. Only your
   signature opens it. You can say "Not yet."
5. Later it may ask for a library. Plain-text public-domain books work,
   for example .txt files from Project Gutenberg in your language.
6. The Record tab shows a line falling as it learns your language.

KEEP IT SAFE
Your seed lives in one browser on one device. Clearing that browser's
site data, or using a private window, erases it. Use Record > Save a
backup now and then. To move it to another device, open the seed there
and choose the backup file under "Already raising one?".
The backup file holds your seed's signing key. Anyone with the file can
sign for your seed. Keep it like a house key.

FEEDBACK
On day 3 and day 10, open Feedback, answer what you like, tap
"Build and copy report", and paste it into a message back to Rusty.
Your conversation with the seed is NOT included unless you tick the box.

KNOWN LIMITS OF THIS BETA
- It learns letter patterns. It will reach word-like and phrase-like
  speech, not real conversation.
- The signing key lives in the same page as the seed, so signatures here
  show the idea, not hardened security.
- One seed per browser.

Adults only for this beta.
